Grilled Peaches with Vanilla Ice Cream

Home

Description

Warm, soft, and smoky, these grilled peaches will blow your mind. Pair them with vanilla icecream for the greatest dessert ever!

Ingredients

  • 2 peaches
  • 2 nectarines
  • Canola oil
  • 8 scoops of vanilla icecream
  • Honey and sea salt for finishing

Steps

  1. Heat grill to medium high heat.
  2. Brush the peaches and nectarines with oil and place on the grill for a few minutes.
  3. Rotate the peaches and nectarines 90 degrees and let cook for about 3-4 minutes total.
  4. Remove the peaches and nectarines from the grill and serve with a scoop of ice cream in the center of the fruit
