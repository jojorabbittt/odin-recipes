Oven Baked Greek Branzino

Home

Description

Branzino, also known as European sea bass, is a light, flaky white fish. It has a subtle sweet taste. This recipe bakes a whole branzino infused with fresh lemon, garlic, and herbs.

Ingredients

  • 1 pound whole branzino
  • Extra virgin olive oil
  • 1/2 a lemon
  • 1/2 a red onion
  • 1/2 cup fresh dill
  • 1 cup cherry tomatoes
  • Ladolemono sauce

Steps

  1. Heat the oven to 400 degrees F.
  2. Pat the fish dry. Score the fish on both sides. Rub the fish with kosher salt and black pepper on both sides, in score marks, and cavity.
  3. Stuff the fish cavity with sliced onions and lemon
  4. Roast in the oven on one side for 5 minutes. Flip and roast the other side for 5 to 7 minutes. Turn the broiler on and place the fish about 6 inches from the heat source until the skin chars
  5. While the fish is cooking prepare the Ladolemono sauce.
  6. As soon as the fish is finished cooking, remove it from the oven. Transfer the fish to a plate and immediately drizzle as much of the Ladolemono sauce as you wish. Toss the cherry tomatoes with a little salt and spoon them over the fish.
