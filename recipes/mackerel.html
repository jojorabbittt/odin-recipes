Shaba Shioyaki (Grilled mackerel)

Home

Description

Grilled mackerel, also known as Shaba Shioyaki in Japan, is a popular dish served in Japanese restaurants.

Ingredients

  • 2 mackerel fillets
  • 2 tablespoons of sake
  • 1/2 teaspoon of kosher salt
  • 1 inch of daikon radish
  • 1 wedge of lemon
  • 1 teaspoon of soy sauce

Steps

  1. Gather all the ingredients. Cut the mackerel fillets in half crosswise.
  2. Coat 2 mackerel filles with 2 tablespoons of sake.
  3. Pat dry with paper towels and transfer the fish to a baking sheet lined with parchment paper.
  4. Sprinkle both sides of the fish with 1/2 tablespoon of kosher salt
  5. Let the fish sit at room temperature for 20 minutes. During this time, preheat the oven to 425 degrees F.
  6. After 20 minutes, pat dry the excess moisture on the fish.
  7. Place the fish skin side down on the parchment paper and bake for 15-20 minutes.
  8. Grate the 1 inch daikon radish and squeeze out most of the liquid.
  9. Serve the grilled mackerel on a plate with the grated daikon and 1 lemon wedge on the side. Pour a few drops of soy sauce on the grated daikon.
